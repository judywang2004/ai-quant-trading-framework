# Why This Project Exists

The purpose of this project is **not** to build software.

The purpose is **not** to build detectors.

The purpose is **not** to build another Expert Advisor.

The purpose is **not** to impress employers.

The purpose of this project is to **discover, validate, compound, and automate profitable trading edge.**

Software is only the vehicle.

Trading Memory is the asset.

Edge is the destination.

---

# Vision

Build an AI-native Quantitative Research Platform that continuously discovers, validates, improves and automates profitable trading strategies.

The platform should combine:

- Human experience
- Rule-based quantitative models
- AI reasoning
- Historical replay validation

into one continuous research loop.

The long-term goal is not to predict markets.

The long-term goal is to build a system that continuously learns from the market and becomes a better trader over time.

# Core Principles

## 1. Research First

Research comes before engineering.

We optimize our understanding of the market before optimizing code.

Every detector, strategy, EA and machine learning model must originate from research.

---

## 2. Opportunity First

We study market opportunities—not trades.

An Opportunity may produce:

- BUY
- SELL
- WAIT
- IGNORE

A correct WAIT is just as valuable as a profitable BUY.

The goal is correct decision making, not maximum trading frequency.

---

## 3. Trading Memory

Every opportunity becomes permanent Trading Memory.

A Trading Memory contains:

- Market Snapshot
- Multi-timeframe Screenshot
- AI Analysis
- Framework Analysis
- Human Analysis
- Trade Proposal
- Replay Outcome
- Reflection
- Research Notes

Trading Memory is the most valuable asset of this project.

Strategies can change.

Models can change.

Trading Memory remains.

---

## 4. Objective Validation

Historical replay outcome is the only objective ground truth.

Claude is not the truth.

Human is not the truth.

Our detector is not the truth.

Replay outcome is the truth.

Every hypothesis must be validated.

---

## 5. AI is a Research Partner

AI is not an oracle.

AI generates hypotheses.

Humans verify hypotheses.

The framework learns from validated hypotheses.

Claude, GPT, Gemini and future models are all Decision Engines—not authorities.

# Trading Memory

↓

Each memory captures:

- Why the decision was made
- Who made the decision
- What happened afterwards
- What was learned

---

# Opportunity Library

The Opportunity Library is a curated collection of Trading Memories.

Not every candle becomes an Opportunity.

Only interesting market situations are recorded.

Each Opportunity answers one question:

> **What is the best trading decision at this moment?**

Multiple Decision Engines answer independently.

Replay reveals the objective outcome.

Over time, the library becomes the foundation for:

- Detector improvement
- Strategy research
- Prompt optimization
- Machine learning
- EA generation

---

# Decision Engines

Every Opportunity may be evaluated by multiple Decision Engines.

Examples:

• Claude Vision

• GPT Vision

• Gemini Vision

• Rule-based Framework

• Human Trader

• Future Machine Learning Models

Each engine produces a Decision Record using the same schema.

Replay determines which decision was objectively better.
